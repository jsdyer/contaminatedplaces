What this map can tell you

Federal responsibility

This is the Federal Contaminated Sites Inventory, including sites where Canada has accepted financial responsibility. It also contains some overseas federal sites, grouped under “Outside Canada / province not reported.” It is not a complete list of provincial, municipal or private contaminated properties.

Read status and stage together

Suspected sites need assessment. Active sites may be in investigation, remediation, risk management or monitoring. Closed records can include sites where no contamination was found. A completed step describes reported work, not a safety certificate.

Check the record date

Source checks run weekly. An older site-modified date or fiscal year is kept as published. Coordinates are approximate and some are missing. Consult the custodian and official record for current conditions.

For year-by-year spending and investigation histories in BC and Nova Scotia, use Toxic Ground.
